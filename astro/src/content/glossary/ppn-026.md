<p class="gl-lead"><strong>Procurement Policy Note 026</strong> is the next edition of the Social Value Model, published on GOV.UK on 5 August 2026. It applies to central government procurements commenced on or after <strong>1 January 2027</strong>, at <strong>1 million pounds inc VAT or above</strong>.</p>
<p>The minimum social value weighting is <strong>10%</strong> from 1 million pounds inc VAT to 5 million, and <strong>20%</strong> at 5 million pounds inc VAT or above. "Or above" is inclusive, so a procurement at exactly 5 million pounds inc VAT sits in the 20% band, and 10% is the minimum anywhere in scope.</p>
<p>Procurements commenced before 1 January 2027 fall under the previous edition of the Social Value Model. A proposal being written today is usually for a procurement that will commence in 2027, so this is the edition most bids in draft now will be scored under.</p>

<h2>What this page states, and what it does not</h2>
<div class="gl-fact">
<ul>
<li><strong>Five facts are cited here and nothing else:</strong> that this is the next edition of the Social Value Model, the publication date of 5 August 2026, the application date of 1 January 2027 for central government procurements, the 1 million pounds inc VAT threshold, and the two minimum weightings. Each is recorded with its source on <a href="/sources">our sources page</a>.</li>
<li><strong>The rest of the scoring model is not summarised here.</strong> A glossary entry on a compliance subject is worth less than nothing if it is filled in from expectation, so the missions and the outcomes stay off this page until each one can carry a source.</li>
<li><strong>The date that decides which edition applies is when the procurement was commenced</strong>, not when the proposal was written. Where the invitation to tender names the model and the weighting, that statement governs the bid in front of you, and it is worth checking per tender rather than per calendar.</li>
</ul>
</div>
