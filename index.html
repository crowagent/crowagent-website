System status

Figures are computed, not written

Every pound and percentage in a generated answer comes from your own confirmed data, calculated in code. Prose containing a figure the data does not support is rejected rather than shown to you.

Evidence is quoted, or dropped

Supporting quotes must be found word for word in the source document. Anything the model cannot locate verbatim is discarded, so a citation can never be invented to fill a gap.

It fails out loud

When the AI cannot run, you get an explicit error and the feature says so. You never get a confident answer that was quietly made up because the real one was unavailable.
