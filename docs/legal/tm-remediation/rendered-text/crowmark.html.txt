Why CrowMark
Three things most
bid tools will not do.
The AI cannot invent a number, every draft says it is AI-assisted, and the commitment you win on is the commitment you report against.
The AI cannot invent a figure
Before a draft is shown, every £ and % in the prose is checked against a set of allowed figures computed from your own data. If a number is not in that set, the draft is rejected rather than published. The language model is never the source of a figure.
PPN 017 disclosure, human approval
Every AI-assisted draft carries a PPN 017 AI-transparency disclosure, and nothing reaches a buyer without a person approving it. If the AI provider is unavailable, CrowMark returns nothing rather than a canned answer.
Award to delivery to reporting
The commitment you bid stays attached to the contract after award: delivery percentages with RAG status, evidence capture, monthly reports and reminders, plus s.52 and s.71 KPI checks under the Procurement Act 2023.
Procurement Act 2023
The KPI duties nobody
reads until it's too late.
CrowMark checks your contract against the Procurement Act 2023 KPI duties and tells you where you stand. Advisory, not legal advice.
Section 52
Public contracts with an estimated value above £5m must have at least three published key performance indicators. CrowMark flags the threshold, counts your published KPIs, and shows the shortfall.
Section 71
Performance against those KPIs has to be assessed and published. CrowMark holds the KPI set alongside your delivery evidence, so the assessment is drawn from the record rather than reconstructed.

UK tender discovery

Grounded answer drafting

Deterministic PPN 002 maths
Social value is calculated in code with unit-aware arithmetic against the 2025 PPN 002 model: five missions, M1 to M5, and eight policy outcomes. Measures are TOMs-aligned. The 10% minimum weighting is checked, never guessed.
Tender document ingestion
PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG and JPEG up to 10MB, with requirement extraction and a 92% grounding floor on every quote. Scanned PDFs are reported honestly as needing OCR, which CrowMark does not do.
Delivery tracking and reports
Commitment delivery percentages with RAG status, evidence capture against each commitment, monthly social-value reports, and monthly evidence reminders. Branded PDF and DOCX exports are generated for real.
Win and loss capture
Record the outcome against structured lost-reason categories, with follow-ups at day 1, day 14 and day 30. Those reasons feed the measures CrowMark suggests on your next bid. AI-drafted proposal diagrams are yours to edit.

Frequently asked questions
What does "beta" mean for CrowMark?
CrowMark is live, paid and used on real bids, and we are still building. Features listed under Available now work today. Features listed under In development are written but not released, and no customer can use them yet. We label the difference rather than blur it.
Where do the tenders come from?
Two UK sources, ingested from their published OCDS APIs: Contracts Finder and Find a Tender. A cron refreshes the feed at 06:00 each day. You filter by sector, geography and contract value, sort the results, work within a 14-day freshness window, and bookmark what you want to come back to. Devolved-nation portals are not covered.
Can the AI make up a number in my bid?
No. Every £ and % that appears in generated prose has to exist in an allowed-figure set computed from your own data before the draft is released. If it does not, the draft is rejected. The language model is never the source of a figure, and the social-value totals are calculated in code with unit-aware arithmetic.
Is AI use disclosed to the buyer?
Yes. Every AI-assisted draft carries a PPN 017 AI-transparency disclosure, and a person has to approve the answer before it is submitted. If the AI provider is unavailable, CrowMark returns nothing rather than a generic fallback answer.
How is social value calculated?
Deterministically, in code. CrowMark uses a curated catalogue of 19 social-value measures, aligned to National TOMs conventions, and applies unit-aware arithmetic against the 2025 PPN 002 model of five missions, M1 to M5, and eight policy outcomes. It is not a full National TOMs implementation, and the AI never computes the total. PPN 002 is dated February 2025 and is mandatory from 1 October 2025, with a 10% minimum social-value weighting.
Can CrowMark read a scanned tender document?
No. CrowMark reads PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG and JPEG files up to 10MB and extracts requirements from the text layer, holding every quote to a 92% grounding floor against the source. A scanned PDF with no text layer is reported as needing OCR, which CrowMark does not perform.
Does CrowMark cover the Procurement Act 2023 KPI duties?
Yes. CrowMark checks section 52, which applies above an estimated contract value of £5m and expects at least three published KPIs, and section 71, on assessing and publishing performance against them. The check is advisory and is not legal advice.
