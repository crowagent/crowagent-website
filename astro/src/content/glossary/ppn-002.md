<div class="gl-tldr">
<h2>TL;DR</h2>
<ul>
<li><span><strong>What it is:</strong> UK Cabinet Office rule on social value in bids, published 13 February 2025, mandatory from 1 October 2025.</span></li>
<li><span><strong>The 10% rule:</strong> at least 10% of the evaluation score goes to social value.</span></li>
<li><span><strong>How you score it:</strong> the TOMs framework with National TOMs 2023-24 proxy values.</span></li>
</ul>
</div>

<p class="gl-lead"><strong>Procurement Policy Note 002</strong> (published 13 February 2025) is a UK Cabinet Office instruction. All central government departments, their executive agencies, and non-departmental public bodies must allocate a <strong>minimum 10% weighting</strong> of the total contract evaluation score to social value.</p>
<p>PPN 002 applies to new procurements. Contracts initiated under PPN 06/20 stay on the earlier framework.</p>

<h2>The 5 PPN 002 missions</h2>
<div class="gl-fact">
<ul>
<li><strong>1. Fighting climate change:</strong> carbon reduction, waste minimisation, sustainable procurement.</li>
<li><strong>2. Tackling economic inequality:</strong> SME supply-chain access, skills development, fair work.</li>
<li><strong>3. Equal opportunity:</strong> inclusive employment, disability representation, progression pathways.</li>
<li><strong>4. Wellbeing:</strong> community integration, mental health, workforce wellbeing.</li>
<li><strong>5. Covid-19 recovery:</strong> (legacy mission, reducing in relevance as recovery progresses).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>Key facts</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Minimum weighting:</strong> 10% of total evaluation score (buyers can set higher).</li>
<li><strong>Scoring framework:</strong> <a href="/glossary/toms-framework">TOMs (Themes, Outcomes, Measures)</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Proxy values:</strong> <strong>National TOMs 2023-24</strong> is the canonical reference.</li>
<li><strong>Scope:</strong> procurements commenced under the Procurement Act 2023 by central government departments, their executive agencies and non-departmental public bodies. Mandatory for those commenced on or after 1 October 2025.</li>
<li><strong>Relation to PPN 06/20:</strong> PPN 002 builds on and supersedes PPN 06/20 (September 2020) for new contracts; PPN 06/20 continues to apply to contracts initiated under its regime.</li>
</ul>
