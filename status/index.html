        What the statuses mean, and the distinction that matters most:

          BUILT is not FIXED. BUILT means the build is green. FIXED additionally means the rendered page was looked at. The owner's complaint on 3 August was precisely that work was called done on a measurement without anyone looking at the pixels, so the two are kept apart here deliberately.

          Statuses: FIXED is implemented and verified. BUILT is implemented, certification pending. WIP is in progress. OPEN is not started. DECISION is waiting on an owner decision. CLEARED is not applicable, the premise was false or the item is counted against another row. Open a row to read its latest evidence.
