<h2>TL;DR</h2>
<ul>
<li><span><strong>What it is:</strong> a UK Cabinet Office Procurement Policy Note, published on GOV.UK on 5 August 2026.</span></li>
<li><span><strong>When it applies:</strong> to central government procurements commenced on or after 1 January 2027.</span></li>
<li><span><strong>Before that date:</strong> <a href="/glossary/ppn-002">PPN 002</a> governs procurements already commenced, and is unaffected by the arrival of this notice.</span></li>
</ul>
</div>

<p class="gl-lead"><strong>Procurement Policy Note 026</strong> was published on GOV.UK on 5 August 2026. It applies to central government procurements commenced on or after <strong>1 January 2027</strong>.</p>

<h2>What this page states, and what it does not</h2>
<div class="gl-fact">
<ul>
<li><strong>Two facts are cited here and nothing else:</strong> the publication date of 5 August 2026, and the application date of 1 January 2027 for central government procurements. Both are recorded with their source on <a href="/sources">our sources page</a>.</li>
<li><strong>This page does not state that PPN 026 replaces or supersedes any earlier notice.</strong> The published note describes a transition period from a previous edition. It does not name PPN 002 as the notice it replaces, so neither does this page. If that relationship is set out on GOV.UK, it will be added here with the citation attached.</li>
<li><strong>The scoring model is not summarised here yet.</strong> A glossary entry on a compliance subject is worth less than nothing if it is filled in from expectation, so the missions, the outcomes and any weighting stay off this page until each one can carry a source.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>Which notice applies to a given bid</h2>
<p>The question is <strong>when the procurement was commenced</strong>, not when the bid is written. A procurement commenced before 1 January 2027 continues under the regime it started in, which for in-scope central government contracts commenced on or after 1 October 2025 is <a href="/glossary/ppn-002">PPN 002</a>. A procurement commenced on or after 1 January 2027 falls to PPN 026. A supplier bidding across the turn of the year can therefore be answering two different notices in the same week, which is the ordinary position during any transition and is worth checking per tender rather than per calendar.</p>
<p>The tender documents are the authority on this. Where a buyer states the model and the weighting in the invitation to tender, that statement governs the bid you are writing.</p>
