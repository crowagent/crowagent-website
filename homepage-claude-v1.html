Deliberate limits

What it will not do.

The constraints are the product. Anything that would put an unsupportable claim in front of an evaluator has been designed out, not left to the writer's judgement.

It never predicts whether you will win.

The fit score measures coverage against your own recorded history. It is decision support for a bid or no bid call, and the product says so on the screen.

It never invents a number.

Figures are computed in code from data you confirmed. Text carrying a figure that cannot be traced is rejected rather than shown.

It never quotes another supplier's bid.

An answer may draw on three sources: the published rules, this tender, and your own answer library. Nothing else is in scope.

It never submits on your behalf.

Nothing is sent to a procurement portal for you, and connectors to your own systems are read only. We do not write back or change a setting.

It never scores on the buyer's behalf.

No evaluation panel is simulated and no mark is set, changed or suggested. The authority evaluates and scores the response.

It never quietly returns nothing.

When the drafter cannot run, you get an explicit error. You do not get a canned answer presented as though the model wrote it.
