## 0. The owner's headline complaint: "headline text showing as cut in bottom"

**I could not reproduce hard clipping of any heading, on any route, at any of the three viewports.** I am stating that plainly rather than quietly omitting it. Here is exactly what was tested and ruled out:

| Mechanism tested | How it was tested | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `overflow: hidden/clip` on the heading or any ancestor cutting the glyph ink | Canvas font metrics (`measureText` → `fontBoundingBoxDescent`, `actualBoundingBoxDescent`) combined with `Range.getClientRects()` to compute the true ink bottom of the last line, compared against every clipping ancestor's padding-box edge. All 43 routes × 3 viewports. | **0 real hits.** 200 raw hits, every one of them `.visually-hidden` / `.sr-only` / honeypot `.form__pot` — i.e. deliberately hidden text. |
| `-webkit-line-clamp` truncation | Computed style sweep, 43 routes × 2 viewports | **0 elements** on the whole site. |
| Fixed height + overflow on a text element | `scrollHeight > clientHeight` with `overflow:hidden`, 43 routes | Only `.visually-hidden` spans and `.amb` ambient decoration layers. No headings. |
| `background-clip: text` gradient box smaller than the glyph ink (descender receives no paint) | Screenshot at DSR 2 of the heading box, pixel row profile via `sharp`, compared against a control with `-webkit-text-fill-color:#fff; background:none` forced on. `/privacy/`, `/blog/`, `/sectors/highways/`. | **Delta = 0.00 px.** Not the cause. `background-repeat` is `repeat`, so the gradient tiles past the box and descenders are painted. |
| A section background band edge cutting through a heading | Every `h1/h2/h3` box vs the top/bottom edge of every painted band ≥60px tall, 43 routes × 3 viewports | **0 hits.** |
| A later element painting over the heading's descender zone | `elementFromPoint` at the descender row, each heading individually scrolled into view first, 43 routes × 2 viewports | 1 hit on `/about/` `"Where we are."`, which on re-measure is a **false positive** — heading bottom 4868.6, first timeline card top 4921.4, a 52.8 px gap. |
| Horizontal page scroll pushing content out of view | `documentElement.scrollWidth − clientWidth`, 43 routes × 3 viewports | **0 px overflow anywhere.** No horizontal scroll on the site. |

**What I did find that most plausibly produces the impression** is finding **TY-01** below: every gradient heading fades from pure white at the top of the glyph to a mid grey at the bottom of the glyph, *within a single line*. Measured peak luminance drops from **255 → 201** across one glyph height. On a descender (`y`, `p`, `g`, `q`), the tail is the greyest part of the letter and reads as if it has been cut away. That is real, measurable, and on every page. It is not clipping, but it looks like it.

If the owner can point at one specific page and viewport, I can pin it in minutes. Everything short of that has been eliminated above.
