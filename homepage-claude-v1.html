The whole lifecycle, not the easy half

Discovery, qualification, drafting and post-award evidencing in one system, built on the Procurement Act 2023 and the National TOMs rather than bolted onto them.

Grounded Answer Drafter

Drafts award answers against the criteria the tender publishes. An answer may draw on three sources only: the published rules, this tender, and your own approved answer library. A named person reviews and approves before anything is submitted.

Contracts Finder & FTS Ingest
