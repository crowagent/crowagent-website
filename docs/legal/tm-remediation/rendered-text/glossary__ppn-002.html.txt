PPN 002
Procurement Policy Note 002.
UK Cabinet Office policy. Sets a mandatory minimum 10% social value weighting on central government contract evaluations.
TL;DR
What it is:
UK Cabinet Office rule (Feb 2025) on social value in bids.
The 10% rule:
at least 10% of the evaluation score goes to social value.
How you score it:
the TOMs framework with Oxford SVB 2023-24 proxy values.
Procurement Policy Note 002
(February 2025) is a UK Cabinet Office instruction. All central government departments, their executive agencies, and non-departmental public bodies must allocate a
minimum 10% weighting
of the total contract evaluation score to social value.
PPN 002 applies to new procurements. Contracts initiated under PPN 06/20 stay on the earlier framework.
The 5 PPN 002 missions
1. Fighting climate change:
carbon reduction, waste minimisation, sustainable procurement.
2. Tackling economic inequality:
SME supply-chain access, skills development, fair work.
3. Equal opportunity:
inclusive employment, disability representation, progression pathways.
4. Wellbeing:
community integration, mental health, workforce wellbeing.
5. Covid-19 recovery:
(legacy mission, reducing in relevance as recovery progresses).
Key facts
Minimum weighting:
10% of total evaluation score (buyers can set higher).
Scoring framework:
TOMs (Themes, Outcomes, Measures)
Proxy values:
Oxford Social Value Bank 2023-24
is the canonical reference.
Scope:
new central government contracts from February 2025.
Relation to PPN 06/20:
PPN 002 builds on and supersedes PPN 06/20 (September 2020) for new contracts; PPN 06/20 continues to apply to contracts initiated under its regime.
