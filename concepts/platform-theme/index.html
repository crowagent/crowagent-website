Controls

Nine platform variants collapse to four here, and that is the finding, not a shortcut. The Button primitive ships primary, secondary, destructive, danger, warn, outline, success, ghost and subtle. Today primary and success paint the same teal fill and danger is a literal alias of destructive, so three of the nine are duplicates before any palette moves. Under V6 the teal fill has to go neutral, which merges primary with success outright and leaves subtle (teal text) with nothing to be.
