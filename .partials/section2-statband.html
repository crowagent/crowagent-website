The UK procurement market

The shape of a UK bid, in four numbers

Public procurement here is not short of opportunity. The pressure sits in the middle of the lifecycle: the qualification threshold, the written answers, and the duties that begin the moment a contract is awarded.

  1. 01  Find

    40,000+

    Tenders published every year

    Across Contracts Finder and Find a Tender. A shortage of opportunities was never the problem.

  2. 02  Qualify

    55%

    Are stopped at the qualification threshold

    Standing, financial checks and past performance, asked again by every buyer, in a different shape each time.

  3. 03  Answer

    44%

    Cite process complexity as the barrier

    Award questions, clarifications and social value all have to be written, and every figure in them has to be evidenced.

  4. 04  Deliver

    £5m

    Where post-award duties begin

    Above this threshold the Procurement Act 2023 sets minimum KPIs under section 52, so what was promised has to be tracked and reported. Advisory, not legal advice.

Notice volumes: Contracts Finder and Find a Tender. Supplier figures: published UK supplier surveys reported by CIPS Supply Management and techUK. Threshold: Procurement Act 2023, section 52. These figures describe the UK procurement market, not this product’s performance.
