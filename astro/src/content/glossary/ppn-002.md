<div class="gl-tldr">
<h2>TL;DR</h2>
<ul>
<li><span><strong>What it is:</strong> UK Cabinet Office rule on social value in bids, published 13 February 2025, mandatory from 1 October 2025.</span></li>
<li><span><strong>The 10% rule:</strong> at least 10% of the evaluation score goes to social value.</span></li>
<li><span><strong>How you score it:</strong> five government missions, eight policy outcomes, and the standard reporting metrics beneath them.</span></li>
</ul>
</div>

<p class="gl-lead"><strong>Procurement Policy Note 002</strong> (published 13 February 2025) is a UK Cabinet Office instruction. All central government departments, their executive agencies, and non-departmental public bodies must allocate a <strong>minimum 10% weighting</strong> of the total contract evaluation score to social value.</p>
<p>It applies to procurements commenced under the Procurement Act 2023 on or after 1 October 2025. For procurements commenced under the Act before that date, buyers could apply PPN 002 or continue with PPN 06/20 during the transition. PPN 06/20 has not been withdrawn.</p>

<h2>The 5 PPN 002 missions</h2>
<div class="gl-fact">
<ul>
<li><strong>M1. Kickstart economic growth:</strong> three outcomes sit here, Fair work, Skills for growth, and Resilient, innovative and flexible supply chains.</li>
<li><strong>M2. Make Britain a clean energy superpower:</strong> one outcome, Sustainable procurement practices.</li>
<li><strong>M3. Take back our streets:</strong> one outcome, Support the reduction in crime through community cohesion.</li>
<li><strong>M4. Break down barriers to opportunity:</strong> two outcomes, Employment and training for those who face barriers to employment, and Creating a pipeline of opportunities for the contract workforce.</li>
<li><strong>M5. Build an NHS fit for the future:</strong> one outcome, Increasing productivity through physical and mental wellbeing.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<p>These are the missions of the current model. COVID-19 recovery, Levelling Up, fighting climate change, equal opportunity and wellbeing were the five themes of the <strong>previous</strong> Social Value Model under PPN 06/20, and are not the PPN 002 missions. If a bid library still uses them, it predates this model.</p>

<h2>Key facts</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Minimum weighting:</strong> 10% of the total score, or an equivalent measurement (buyers can set higher).</li>
<li><strong>Structure:</strong> five missions, eight policy outcomes, model award criteria beneath each outcome, standard reporting metrics beneath those.</li>
<li><strong>How buyers use it:</strong> the buyer selects an outcome relevant to the subject matter of the contract. The Cabinet Office guide encourages selecting a single outcome except on the largest contracts.</li>
<li><strong>Units, not money:</strong> the reporting metrics are counts, hours, litres, tonnes, square metres and pounds of direct spend. PPN 002 does not use monetary proxy values, and does not mention <a href="/glossary/toms-framework">TOMs</a>, which is a separate framework published by the Social Value Portal and required by many buyers outside central government.</li>
<li><strong>Additionality:</strong> social value must be over and above the core deliverables of the tender or the contract.</li>
<li><strong>Scope:</strong> procurements commenced under the Procurement Act 2023 by central government departments, their executive agencies and non-departmental public bodies. Mandatory for those commenced on or after 1 October 2025.</li>
<li><strong>Relation to PPN 06/20:</strong> PPN 002 replaces PPN 06/20 (September 2020) for procurements in scope from 1 October 2025. PPN 06/20 is not withdrawn and continues to apply to procurements commenced under its regime.</li>
</ul>
